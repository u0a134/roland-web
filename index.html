Signs your home needs a closer look

Most roof and siding problems start small and quiet. If you notice any of these, a free inspection now costs a lot less than a rotten deck later.

Book a free inspection

  • Missing or curling shingles

    Wind and age lift the edges, and every gap is a path for water.

  • Stains on ceilings or walls

    By the time it shows inside, water has usually been getting in for a while.

  • Granules in the gutters

    Shingles shedding their grit are near the end of their life.

  • Overflowing gutters

    Clogged or undersized gutters soak the fascia and feed ice dams in winter.

  • Sagging or soft spots

    A dip in the roof line often means the deck underneath is rotting.

  • Cracked or loose siding

    Water gets behind the wall and works quietly for months before you see it.

Common questions

The things homeowners ask us most. If yours isn't here, send it on WhatsApp and we'll answer the same day.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the roof, siding or gutters, and give you a written estimate at no cost and with no obligation to book the work.

How quickly can you give me a price?

Usually within a day of seeing the job. For smaller repairs, a couple of clear photos on WhatsApp is often enough for us to give you a good idea straight away.

Do I need a repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the age of the roof, how many layers it has and where it's failing. Isolated wind damage or one bad flashing is a repair. If your roof still has good years left, we'll tell you so.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. After storm damage we document the roof with photos and measurements you can give your adjuster, and we'll meet them on site if that helps.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Roland Contracting LLC is registered in Pennsylvania under PA 6855562 and carries liability insurance on every job.

How long does a new roof take?

Most single-family homes take one to three days, depending on size, pitch and how many layers are coming off. You'll have the number before we start.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes, small commercial buildings as well as houses and row homes, including the flat and low-slope roofs common on them.
