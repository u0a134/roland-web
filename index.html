About us

Roland Contracting LLC is a roofing company based in Pittsburgh, PA. We work on houses, row homes and small commercial buildings across the city and the surrounding boroughs, and we've spent long enough on local roofs to know what the winter here does to flashing, valleys and gutters.

Our vision is to be a trusted leader in roofing and contracting, recognized for excellence, integrity and commitment to our customers. At Roland Contracting LLC, we strive to deliver durable solutions and build lasting relationships with our clients that exceed expectations on every project.
