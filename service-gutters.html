Gutters

Seamless gutters, downspouts and guards, sized and pitched so water leaves the building instead of sitting on it.

Gutters are the cheapest part of a house to get wrong and one of the most expensive to ignore. Undersized, badly pitched, or packed with shingle grit, they overflow at the fascia, soak the board behind them, freeze solid at the eaves, and eventually push meltwater back under the bottom courses of shingle.

We roll seamless aluminium gutter to length on site. That means no joints along the run, and nothing sitting there waiting to split open in February.

Gutter services we offer

  • Seamless installation

    Aluminium gutter rolled to length on site, with no joints along the run to leak or split.

  • Gutter replacement

    Undersized, sagging or rusted gutters taken down and replaced with a system sized to your roof.

  • Repair and re-pitching

    Loose hangers, leaking corners and gutters that hold water, adjusted and sealed so they drain again.

  • Guards and downspouts

    Guards where trees make them worth it, and downspouts routed well away from your foundation.

What the job includes

What goes into a gutter run:

  • Seamless aluminium in 5 or 6 inch — rolled to the exact length of your run in the driveway, so there are no field seams to fail.
  • Correct pitch — roughly a quarter inch of fall for every ten feet, set with a level rather than by eye.
  • Hidden hangers into the rafter tails — instead of spikes driven through tired fascia board.
  • Downspouts sized to the roof area — and routed far enough out that the water isn't returned to your foundation.
  • Gutter guards where they earn their keep — and an honest 'you don't need these' where they don't.
  • Fascia repair — if the board behind the old gutter has gone soft, because new gutter on rotten wood is a waste of your money.

How the work runs

  1. Measure the roof, not the old gutter

    Gutter size follows roof area and pitch. What was there before may have been undersized from the day it went up.

  2. Check the fascia and the ground

    We look at the board behind the gutter and at where the downspouts have been dumping water for the last decade.

  3. You get it in writing

    Linear feet, gutter size, number of downspouts, guards if any, and the price.

  4. Remove and roll

    The old run comes down, and the new one is rolled to length on site while you watch.

  5. Hang, connect, flood test

    We run water through the finished system and watch where it goes before we call the job done.

Questions we get asked

Five inch or six inch?

Five inch handles most city houses. Six inch is worth it on steep pitches, large roof areas, or where a lot of roof drains into one short run, which is common on hillside properties. We size it off the measurements rather than defaulting to whatever came off.

Are gutter guards worth the money?

Under mature trees, yes, they'll cut your cleaning down a lot. On an open lot with no overhang, they mostly buy you a bill. We'll say which one you are.

How long does it take?

Most houses are half a day to a full day, including tear-off and haul-away of the old run.

Overflowing at the corners?

Take a video during the next real rain and send it over. Where it spills tells us most of what we need to know.
